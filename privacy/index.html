Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

What this website collects

Your email address, and only that, when you join the waitlist. The waitlist form asks for an email so we can send your invite. This website runs no AI on a server, and it does not collect your prompts, your code, or your API keys.

How we use it

Your email is used to send your waitlist invite and occasional launch updates about Vokrew. That is the whole list. There is no drip campaign.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, pool, or share your email with anyone else.
  • We never use your email to advertise other products.

Storage

Waitlist emails are stored in our waitlist system until invites are fully rolled out or you ask us to remove yours, whichever comes first.

Leaving the list

Reply to any email we send and ask to be removed, and we will delete your address. Every waitlist email includes the same offer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
