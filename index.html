Questions

Asked straight, answered straight.

Is free really free?
Yes. Routes run on your free keys, your local models, and capped hosted lanes first — in that spirit, every time. Paid only fires when you say so: Vokrew asks before it ever spends a cent of yours.
Do you pool other people's free accounts?
No. Never. Your keys are yours, your quota is yours, and nobody else's usage touches it. Nothing is pooled, shared, or resold — that's how platforms get shut down, and it's not a foundation we'd build on.
Which models?
Dozens of free routes at the router level — 36 today — plus the major providers when you bring your own keys, and your local Ollama models. The roster rotates as free lanes open and close; the router tracks it so you don't have to.
Coding only?
Coding first — that's where the crew earns its keep today. The chat face follows on the same core: same router, same ledger, same stamps, its own separate budget.
